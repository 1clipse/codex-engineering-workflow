## Plan Tree Long-Term Project Management

Use `$plan-tree` as the planning authority for roadmap work, requirement clarification, scope or implementation strategy, execution-readiness checks, progress tracking, handoff, decisions, open questions, and plan-to-landing evidence.

Plan Tree governs planning state; it does not grant permission for commits, pushes, releases, destructive actions, or unrelated scope expansion.
